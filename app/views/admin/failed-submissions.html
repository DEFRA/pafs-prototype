How to manage failed submissions

Check if the proposal has arrived in PoL

  • If YES, click on the 'Proposal in PoL' link. The proposal will disappear from the list, end of process.
  • If NO, click on the 'Resend to PoL' link.
    • If it displays successfully resubmitted, end of process.
    • If it displays failed to resubmit, the resend was unsuccessful.
      • For unsuccessful resends, please contact the Program Framework helpdesk helpdesk@programframework.com
      • Also copy into the email the Asset Management Business Systems Team AssetManagementTeam@environment-agency.gov.uk
