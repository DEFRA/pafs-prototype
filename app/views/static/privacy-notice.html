Project Application and Funding Service Privacy Notice

How we use your personal data

We are the Environment Agency and we run the Project Application and Funding service. We are the data controller for this service. A data controller determines how and why personal data (personal information) is processed.

Our personal information charter explains how we deal with your personal information. Go to GOV.UK and search 'Environment Agency personal information charter'.

The personal data we need

The personal data we process includes:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Anonymous feedback you leave
  • Information on how you use the site, including cookies

We are allowed to process your personal data to carry out a task in the public interest to manage flood risk. The lawful basis for processing your personal data is to perform a task in the public interest that is set out in law.

If you do not give us this personal data we will not be able to register you as a user on the Project Application and Funding Service.

Site Analytics and Cookies

All data on this site is anonymised. Please see the following cookies link for further information.

What we do with your personal data

We do not use your data to make an automated decision or for automated profiling.

We will not share or disclose your personal data to any party outside the Environment Agency without your explicit consent unless we are lawfully able to do so.

How long we keep your personal data

User data is reviewed every year to ensure that we do not hold on to personal information any longer than is necessary.

Where your personal data is processed and stored

We store your personal data on our servers in the UK.

Contact Details

Our Data Protection Officer (DPO) is responsible for independent advice and monitoring of the Environment Agency's use of personal information.

If you have any concerns or queries about how we process personal data, or if you would like to make a complaint or request relating to data protection, please contact our DPO:
