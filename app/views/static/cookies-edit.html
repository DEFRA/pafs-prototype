Cookie settings on Project Application and Funding Service

Cookies are files saved on your phone, tablet or computer when you visit a website.

We use cookies to store information about how you use the Project Application and Funding Service website.

Cookie settings

We use 2 types of cookie. You can choose which cookies you're happy for us to use.

Strictly necessary cookies (session)

These essential cookies do things like remember your progress through a form.

They will always be on.

_pafs_session - Allows you to log in and manage your account (expires when you close your browser)

cookies_preferences_set - Lets us know whether you've already set your cookies preferences (expires 1 year)

cookies_policy - Lets us know what your cookies preferences are (expires 1 year)

Find out more about cookies on GOV.UK

Cookies that measure website use

We use Google Analytics to measure how you use the website so we can improve it based on user needs. We do not allow Google to use or share the data about how you use this site.

Google Analytics sets cookies that store anonymised information about:

  • how you got to the site
  • the pages you visit on the Project Application and Funding Service, and how long you spend on each page
  • what you click on while you're visiting the site
