Here are simple steps to draw your area in Magic Map

Follow these simple steps to create and upload your polygon:

  1. Click on the Mapping tool link above to open the Magic Map tool.
  2. Use the search field on the left hand side and zoom in on your area of benefit. The arrow pointing downwards allows you to choose your preferred method to zoom in (click on map, drawing box etc)
  3. Click on the 'Drawing Tool' icon.
  4. Click on the Polygon icon.
  5. Now click to start your first vertices and then select to draw your polygon.
  6. Click to start again if you want to draw more shapes.
  7. Double click to complete your shape.
  8. If you want to start again you can select the rubbish bin icon in the Sketch Styler menu.
  9. When you are happy with the shape that you have drawn select on the polygon, it should go blue with an orange box around it.
  10. Now open the Sketch Graphics menu within the Sketch Styler menu and select Save.
  11. The Shape file ZIP will download to your machine.
  12. Go to your computers download folder, to view your file, at this point you can rename it.
  13. Return to PAFS, then upload and continue.
